# Benchmark Analysis

Use this when the user provides a competitor/benchmark account, link, or screenshots.

## Analyze These Elements

1. Account positioning
2. Topic category
3. First-card hook
4. Swipe reason
5. Emotional trigger
6. Visual system
7. Text density
8. Repetition pattern
9. Caption style
10. Comment/share mechanism
11. What can be adapted
12. What should not be copied

## Common Viral Mechanics

### Nostalgia

People comment because they want to place themselves in the memory.

Signals:

- Year-based topics
- childhood/school references
- old brands or apps
- "remember this?" framing

### Identity

People share because the post says something about who they are.

Signals:

- generation labels
- local culture
- fandoms
- food preferences

### Debate

People comment because there is no single correct answer.

Signals:

- ranking
- "best/worst"
- "which one"
- 호불호

### Curiosity List

People swipe because they want to complete the set.

Signals:

- "7 things"
- timeline
- then vs now
- trend history

## Apply To Food Accounts

For a food/recipe account, adapt these mechanics into:

- 추억 간식
- 편의점 신상/트렌드
- SNS 인기 레시피
- 에어프라이어 간식
- 학교 앞 분식
- 홈카페 유행
- 재료 활용법

The post should build the food account's identity, not become a random meme/news page.
